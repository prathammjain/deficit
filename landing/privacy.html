Grain

Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Grain is a food, training and bodyweight tracker at getgrain.fit and app.getgrain.fit, run from Indore, India. This page says what Grain collects, why, who it is shared with, and what you can do about it. Questions go to privacy@getgrain.fit.

What Grain collects

  • Your account. When you sign in with Google, your email address, name and profile picture from Google.
  • Your profile. Sex, age, height, weight, activity level and goal, which set your calorie and protein targets.
  • What you log. Meals (what you wrote, the foods, amounts, calories and macros), weigh ins, workouts (exercises, sets, weights, reps, notes), routines, and programs you write or follow.
  • Voice notes. When you speak a meal or a workout, the recording is sent to our speech to text provider to be written out. Grain does not keep the recording.
  • Your agreement. When and to which version of these terms you agreed.

Grain shows no ads and uses no advertising or tracking cookies. Some data is kept on your phone so the app works without a connection.

Why

  • To work out your targets, log your food and training, and show your progress.
  • To make Grain's estimates better: the meals you describe are stored with a scrambled reference instead of your name or email, and are used to test and improve how meals are read.
  • To keep the service running, secure and free of abuse.

Who it is shared with

  • Services that run Grain: Supabase (accounts and database), Vercel (the website and app) and Google (sign in).
  • AI providers: the text of meals you describe and your voice notes are sent to an AI model provider (currently Groq) to be read. Foods are also looked up in USDA FoodData Central, which receives food names only.
  • A program's author, only if you choose. When you follow a program you can let its author see a summary of your progress: workouts done against the plan, how many days you logged food, your average calories and protein, and your bodyweight change. Never your individual meals. You can stop sharing at any time from the program screen, and the summary is removed when you do.
  • Brands: never without asking. Grain does not sell your data. If Grain ever works with a brand on something that involves your data, you will be asked first, separately, and can say no.
  • The law: if a valid legal request requires it.

These providers may store data on servers outside India. They handle it only to provide their service to Grain.

How long it is kept

Your data is kept while you have an account. When you delete your account, everything tied to it is deleted. Meal descriptions already stored without your name for improving estimates cannot be traced back to you and may be kept.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: everything you log can be viewed and edited in the app.
  • Download: Profile, Download my data, gives you a copy of everything as one file.
  • Delete: Profile, Delete my account, removes your account and everything in it.
  • Withdraw consent: stop sharing with a program's author at any time, or delete your account.
  • Complain: write to privacy@getgrain.fit. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Adults only

Grain is for people aged 18 and over. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone younger, and deletes it on finding out.

Not medical advice

Grain's numbers are estimates. They are not medical advice. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have a history of an eating disorder, talk to a doctor before changing how you eat or train.

Changes

When this policy changes in a way that matters, the date above changes and the app asks you to agree again.
