Targets that adjust to your real burn

Calorie formulas are a starting guess, and they are often off by a few hundred calories. Once a week Grain compares what you ate with how your weight moved, works out what you really burn, and moves your targets toward it. It moves at most 250 kcal a week, so one odd week can’t throw your plan off. One tap keeps last week’s target.

How we decide what goes into Grain

We build on published research, not trends. A rule goes into Grain when the evidence for it is strong, and we keep the source next to the code. When the research is still mixed, we wait.

Evidence: strong

Protein

Gains in lean mass level off at about 1.6 g of protein per kg of bodyweight a day. Grain’s default target is 1.8 g per kg, inside the range the research supports.

Morton et al., British Journal of Sports Medicine, 2018

Evidence: strong

Calories

A formula gives the first target. After that, your real burn comes from what you ate and how your weight moved, which is why your targets update every week.

Energy balance, with the Mifflin St Jeor formula to start

Evidence: strong

Training volume

More weekly sets per muscle build more muscle, with smaller gains as the sets go up. Grain counts your hard sets per muscle each week.

Pelland et al., 2026

Questions

Is Grain free?

Yes, Grain is free right now. A paid plan will come later, and anything you log stays yours either way.

Who is Grain for?

People who depend on their training: athletes, gym professionals, bodybuilders, HYROX racers, coaches and fitness creators. It works for anyone who wants accurate numbers, whether you are losing fat, maintaining or building muscle.

How does Grain count calories?

Tell GrainAI what you ate in plain words, typed or spoken. It splits the meal into foods, looks each one up in nutrition databases, picks the closest match and works out the calories, protein, carbs and fat for your portion. Every food is marked as a good match, an estimate, or an amount to check. You can also search foods or type in your own numbers.

Why is GrainAI invite only?

We are opening GrainAI to small groups so every meal gets an accurate answer while we grow. Request an invite on this page, then sign up with the same email. Search, custom foods, targets and training are open to everyone now.

Can Grain count calories from a photo?

No, on purpose. A photo can’t see the oil in the pan, the chicken under the rice, or how much is really on the plate. Saying what you ate is faster for anyone who already knows, and more accurate.

How do my targets change?

Your first target comes from a formula. Once a week Grain compares what you ate with how your weight moved and moves your targets toward what you really burn, at most 250 kcal at a time. A card shows what changed and why, and one tap keeps last week’s target.

Can I log workouts and HYROX in Grain?

Yes. Workouts fill in last session’s weights and reps, with records and a rest timer. HYROX sims log each run and station for open, pro and doubles divisions, with your trend, records and a race countdown. Both count toward the same weekly record.

Can I import my history from Strong or Hevy?

Yes. Import a Strong or Hevy export, any training spreadsheet saved as CSV, or pasted notes. Exercise names are matched for you.

What if I forget to log a day?

Go back to that day and fill it in, any time later. A day you never log counts as nothing, never as a deficit, so your totals show what you really did.

Does Grain work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Grain runs at app.getgrain.fit. Add it to your home screen and it opens like any other app, works offline and updates itself. On an iPhone, Grain shows you the exact taps.

Does Grain know food from my country?

Grain understands meals from everywhere, home cooking included. Describe a dish the way you would to a friend and it works out each part. Home dishes vary from kitchen to kitchen, so those are marked as estimates.

Is my data private?

Your meals are saved without your name. You can download everything you’ve logged, or delete your account and all of it, at any time.
